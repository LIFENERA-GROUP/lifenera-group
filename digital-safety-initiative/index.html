Who we are

Digital safety should be understandable and accessible.

LIFENERA Digital Safety Initiative is a programme of LIFENERA GROUP, based in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, Nigeria. We help individuals understand common digital threats, take sensible steps to protect their accounts and navigate legitimate support options when something goes wrong.

Our work is centred on education, responsible guidance and helping affected users organise the information needed to report suspected account compromise to the relevant platform.

Our mission

To make digital spaces safer by educating users, supporting people affected by account compromise and helping them access legitimate platform reporting and recovery channels.


Our vision

A more informed digital community where individuals and businesses can use online services with greater confidence and security.

What we do

Practical support, built around safer digital habits.

We focus on helping people understand their options and take the next appropriate step.

⛨

Account compromise guidance

Help users understand suspected account takeovers and identify immediate steps to protect their phone number, email and other accounts.

↻

Official recovery guidance

Explain platform-provided recovery procedures and help users navigate the relevant official support routes.

▤

Case documentation

Help affected users organise a clear incident summary and relevant non-secret details for an official report.

◉

Digital safety education

Share practical information about two-step verification, privacy settings, phishing and safer account practices.

⚑

Online fraud awareness

Raise awareness about impersonation, fake support accounts, suspicious links and social engineering.

⇧

Platform escalation advocacy

Seek authorised routes for forwarding documented cases to platform teams. Any escalation service depends on the platform granting approval.

How assistance works

A careful process, with the account owner in control.

We do not take over accounts or ask clients to share credentials. The account owner remains responsible for completing platform verification.

STEP 01

Make an enquiry

Tell us the platform involved and describe the issue without sharing passwords or verification codes.

STEP 02

Understand the case

We clarify the issue and explain which official recovery or reporting route may apply.

STEP 03

Use official support

We guide the account owner through the platform's own process and any authorised reporting channel available.

STEP 04

Secure the account

When access is restored, we share preventive guidance to help reduce the risk of another compromise.

Important: LIFENERA is an independent initiative and is not currently an authorised WhatsApp or Meta support partner. We cannot access platform systems, override security checks, guarantee account restoration or make ownership decisions. Any future escalation arrangement will only operate if formally authorised by the platform.

Our commitment

Trust begins with responsible handling.

We are committed to clear boundaries and careful treatment of the people who contact us.

Consent and ownership

We only guide or document a case at the request of the affected account owner or an appropriately authorised representative.

No passwords or codes

We never need a user's password, one-time verification code, two-step PIN or recovery code to provide basic guidance.

Honest expectations

We do not claim affiliation with platforms without approval, promise recovery or charge for access to a platform's internal systems.
