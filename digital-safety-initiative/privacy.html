Privacy Notice

LIFENERA Digital Safety Initiative is a programme of LIFENERA GROUP, Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, Nigeria.

This notice explains the limited information involved when you use the initial assistance enquiry form on this website.

How the enquiry form works

The assistance form sends the fields you submit to FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, which forwards the submission to lifeneragroup@gmail.com. Your browser does not open your email application. FormSubmit processes the submitted information to deliver the message; please review FormSubmit's own privacy terms before submitting. This website does not provide a secure case-management portal and does not accept file attachments through the form.

Information to avoid sending

Do not send passwords, one-time verification codes, two-step verification PINs, recovery codes, full payment details or identity documents through the enquiry form or ordinary email. LIFENERA does not need your login credentials to provide basic guidance.

How enquiries are handled

If you submit the form, the information will be received in the LIFENERA GROUP email account and may be used to respond to your enquiry. FormSubmit also handles the information as part of email delivery. Do not send information about another person's account unless you are authorised to act for that person.

Third-party platforms

LIFENERA is independent and is not currently an authorised WhatsApp or Meta support partner. Account verification, investigation and recovery decisions are made by the relevant platform under its own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact lifeneragroup@gmail.com.
